What we install

The short answer

We install water softeners, under-sink reverse osmosis systems, whole-home carbon filters, salt-free conditioners and well water treatment trains across the East Valley — at flat published rates, on any brand, including equipment you bought yourself. We also repair and replace systems other companies installed.

Water Softener Installation

Water softener installation in the East Valley costs $495–$645 when your home already has a softener loop, and $950–$1,650 when a loop has to be built.

Under-sink reverse osmosis installation costs $245–$395 flat in the East Valley and takes about two hours.

Whole-Home Water Filtration

A whole-home carbon filter installs ahead of your softener and removes chlorine, chloramine, and the taste and smell that come with them at every tap and shower in the house.

Prices and detail →

Water Softener Replacement & Removal

A water softener swap-out — old unit removed, new unit installed on the same connections — is $375–$495 and takes about 90 minutes, including hauling away and recycling the old tank.

Well Water Treatment

Well water in Queen Creek, San Tan Valley and Gold Canyon typically needs sediment, iron and sulphur treatment before a softener will work properly.
