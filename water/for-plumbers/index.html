The short answer

We subcontract water softener, reverse osmosis and filtration installations to licensed, insured Arizona plumbing contractors across the East Valley. Rates are posted publicly — $300 for a standard softener install, $225 for a swap-out, $150 for an under-sink RO — and paid by ACH within 7 days of completion.

Publishing what we pay is unusual. We do it because it is the same argument we make to homeowners: a price you can see is a price you can trust. A homeowner who knows the plumber is paid $300 out of a $595 invoice understands exactly what they are buying, and a contractor who can read the rate before calling does not waste an afternoon finding out it was $120 and a promise.

What you get that a lead service does not give you

How a job reaches you

A homeowner sends photos of the space. We confirm the scope, fix the price in writing and collect the address, the equipment details, the access notes and a two-hour window. You get all of it in one message, and you accept or pass — there is no penalty for passing and no minimum.

On completion you send a photo of the finished install and the water test reading. We invoice the homeowner, and your payment goes out by ACH within 7 days. If the job on site is not the job we described, you get the $75 trip fee and we re-quote it — you are never asked to absorb a scope we got wrong.

What we ask

  • A current Arizona ROC licence in a plumbing or water treatment classification, and general liability insurance. We verify both once and keep them on file.
  • Your own truck, tools and materials for standard installs. Fittings and connectors are on you and are priced into the rate.
  • Arrive inside the window, or tell us early enough that we can tell the homeowner.
  • Do not sell on our jobs. If the customer needs something more, tell us and we will quote it — and you will get that work too.

Why we are not hiring employees

Because the work is seasonal and lumpy, and because a plumber who already has a business does this better than someone we trained last month. Our margin is the booking, the scope and the customer relationship. The trade is yours.
