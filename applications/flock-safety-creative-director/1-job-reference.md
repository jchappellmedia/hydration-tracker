## Plain-English summary

This is senior creative leadership, not a production job. You own the creative concept and the craft bar across three disciplines at once — design, film, and motion — for a company turning surveillance and public-safety technology into brand storytelling.

Day to day you are directing integrated campaigns and flagship brand films from concept to delivery, setting the visual and editorial standards that give the company a recognizable look, mentoring discipline leads (art direction, storytelling, critique), and building the systems — reference libraries, templates, guidelines — that make a creative team faster.

The posting is unusually clear about what it is not: not a hands-off review role, not template production management, not pure brand strategy. They want someone who shapes concepts and also gets into the edit and the art execution.
