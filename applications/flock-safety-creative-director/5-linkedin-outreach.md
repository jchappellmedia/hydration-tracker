# LinkedIn Outreach — Creative Director, Flock Safety

At this level, outreach matters more than the application form, not less. A Creative Director hire at a company with $1B raised gets hundreds of applicants and is often filled through conversation. A cold form submission is the weakest possible route.

Search LinkedIn for **"Flock Safety"**, filter by People, then by title. Atlanta and Boston are the hubs.

## Who to target

1. **VP of Brand / VP of Marketing / Head of Creative** — this role reports here. The only person who can decide you are interesting despite the title gap.
2. **Creative Recruiter / Technical Recruiter** — Flock is hiring at scale with 1,700 people. They will tell you straight whether remote is possible and what the level expects.
3. **Existing Senior Producer or Art Director on the team** — the honest read on what the team actually needs.

> I cannot browse LinkedIn from here. Send me names and titles and I will tailor each message.

## Messages

**To the VP of Brand or Head of Creative**

> Hi [Name] — I am applying for the Creative Director role. My pitch is narrow: I own concept through delivery on brand films, and I build the systems that make creative teams faster — a Frame.io library that cut retrieval time 50%, a grading pipeline 3x faster, AI workflows that halved production time. 200+ films delivered, teams up to 20. Reel: vimeo.com/1103241704. Worth a conversation?

**To a recruiter**

> Hi [Name] — I am interested in the Creative Director role for brand, film, and motion. Two quick questions before I go further: is this role open to remote, or is Atlanta/Boston a hard requirement? And what does the team structure look like underneath it? I am a filmmaker with seven years owning campaigns end to end and a strong production-systems track record. Happy to send my reel.

**To a producer or art director on the team**

> Hi [Name] — I applied for the Creative Director opening and wanted to hear from someone actually doing the work. What does the creative group look like day to day, and how much of the role is in the edit versus in review? Reel if you are curious: vimeo.com/1103241704.

## Notes

- **Send the recruiter message first.** It answers the relocation question, which decides whether any of this is worth pursuing.
- **Lead with systems, not taste.** Every applicant will claim taste. The measurable pipeline improvements are the thing that makes a busy VP stop scrolling.
- Keep the demo reel in every message. At this level the work is the entire argument.
- Do not oversell the team-leadership scope in a DM. If it comes up, the honest framing in the analysis document is the one to use.
