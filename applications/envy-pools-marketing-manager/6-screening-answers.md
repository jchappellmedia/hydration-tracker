## 8. Look at our Instagram (@envypoolsaz). What is the first thing you would change, and why?

`NEEDS YOU` — **I cannot see Instagram from this environment; it is blocked.** You have to
look yourself. Do not let me guess, and do not skip this one: it is the question that
separates people who applied from people who prepared.

Spend ten minutes on the account and look for these specific things, which is where pool and
construction brands almost always leave money:

- **Do the Reels open on a finished shot or on a build-up?** Most pool accounts open on a
  slow drone push toward an empty yard. The reveal should be the first frame, not the last.
- **Is there any process content?** Excavation, shotcrete, tile going in, the water turning
  on for the first time. Process is the highest-retention content in construction and the
  most under-used.
- **Are the captions doing any work,** or are they emoji and hashtags? A caption is where the
  lead actually converts.
- **Is the grid consistent in color?** Pool content lives or dies on how the water reads.
  Inconsistent white balance across posts makes a premium brand look like a contractor.
- **Is anyone on camera?** Designers and crew on camera outperform product-only content, and
  they are free to shoot.
- **Is there a single piece of content doing better than the rest,** and has it been built on?

Then write one change, not five. One specific change with a reason beats a list every time.
Format your answer like this:

> The first thing I would change is **[ONE SPECIFIC THING]**. Right now [WHAT YOU OBSERVED].
> I would [THE CHANGE] because [WHAT IT WOULD DO — retention, saves, shares, leads].
> I would test it on [N] posts over two weeks and compare against the current baseline
> before rolling it out.

Send me what you see and I will tighten the wording.
