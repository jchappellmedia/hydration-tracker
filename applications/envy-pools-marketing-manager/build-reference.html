Full job description

Most marketing jobs ask you to make ordinary work look interesting. We have the opposite problem. Envy Pools builds lazy rivers, grottos, massive rock waterfalls and multimillion-dollar backyards across the Phoenix metro, and our marketing has not caught up to what we build. We are hiring the person who closes that gap.

About Envy Pools

Envy Pools is a luxury pool and outdoor living design-build company founded in 2018. We work with homeowners, custom homebuilders and developers on resort-style pools, custom water features, architectural structures and complete outdoor environments. We are a two-time 2026 Pinnacle Award winner, and we are growing.

See the work: www.envypools.com and @envypoolsaz on Instagram.

The role

You will own Envy's marketing: the strategy, the content, the channels and the results. This is not a posting job. One day you are on an active build with a camera. The next you are cutting a reveal, planning a campaign, reviewing ad performance, sitting down with a builder who should be sending us projects, or deciding with ownership what to push next.

What you'll do

  • Set the marketing and social media strategy and run it across Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube and LinkedIn
  • Work with in-house media who photo and video on active job sites and finished projects, from excavation to final reveal
  • Produce Reels, TikToks, YouTube Shorts and long-form project films built on strong hooks and real storytelling
  • Write captions and copy that drive reach, engagement and qualified inquiries
  • Get out and build relationships in person with custom home builders, architects, designers and realtors, and turn them into a [unreadable]
  • Represent Envy at industry events, builder [unreadable]

[not captured in screenshots]

  • Direct outside videographers, photographers and designers when a project calls for it
  • Run paid advertising, or manage the specialists who do, and answer for the return
  • Track audience growth, traffic, leads and conversion, then report what is working and change what is not
  • Produce award submissions, magazine features and co-marketing with our partners
  • Use AI and automation tools to work faster without lowering the standard

What you bring

  • A portfolio of video and social campaigns you personally shot, edited or ran, with results you can show
  • Hands-on photography, videography and editing skills (Premiere Pro, Lightroom, Photoshop, CapCut, Canva or comparable)
  • A working understanding of how Instagram and TikTok distribute content: hooks, retention, shares
  • Strong writing
  • Working knowledge of paid social, marketing funnels, lead tracking and analytics
  • A track record of bringing in business face to face: partnerships, referral relationships, events
  • Self-drive: you set the plan, run several projects at once and don't wait to be told
  • Comfort working on active construction sites outdoors in Arizona, including early-morning and dusk shoots
  • A valid driver's license and reliable transportation to job sites across the Valley

A degree is not required. Proof of work is.

Nice to have

  • Experience marketing luxury, construction, architecture, real estate, hospitality or automotive brands
  • Meta Ads experience with lead-generation results you can point to
  • FAA Part 107 drone certification
  • Motion graphics, color grading or sound design

How success is measured

By outcomes, not post count: qualified reach and audience growth, watch time and shares, website traffic, qualified inquiries, marketing-attributed leads, referral partners brought on, return on ad spend, and signed revenue from projects marketing brought in.

Why this one is different

  • The material. Dramatic construction, complex engineering and finished environments that most creators never get access to.
  • Direct line to ownership. You work with the people who make the decisions. Good ideas move fast.
  • Room to build. The goal is to make Envy one of the most recognized luxury pool builders in the country. You would be building that, not maintaining an account.

Compensation and benefits

  • $65,000–$90,000 a year, based on experience
  • Health insurance, paid time off and paid holidays
  • Full-time, in person: based at our Gilbert office, with shoots on job sites across the Phoenix metro

What employees say: pros and cons

Pros

  • The business checks out: it lists an Arizona contractor license (ROC #332157 KA-5), and BuildZoom shows a permit history and a high contractor score. It has operated since 2018, so it is not a brand-new startup. [1] [2]
  • Customer reviews (not employee reviews) are mostly positive. Chamber of Commerce shows 4.7 from 12 reviews, and the Google reviews on the company site praise design help, clean job sites and communication during the build. The sample is small. [1] [2]
  • The product gives a marketer strong visual material: lazy rivers, grottos, rock waterfalls, infinity edges, high-budget backyards, and 3D design renderings before construction. [1] [2] [3]
  • Per the posting summary, the role owns the content calendar and the social and acquisition strategy (PPC, paid search, paid social, Instagram, Facebook). That is broad ownership, not a narrow task role. [1] [2]
  • The company already does SEO-style content (city landing pages, a blog post on luxury pool features in Arizona), so there is a base to build on, not a blank slate. [1] [2] [3]
  • The office is in Gilbert (85297), a short commute from Mesa, and the posted benefits include health insurance and paid time off. [1] [2]

Cons

  • There are no employee reviews on Glassdoor, Indeed, Comparably, CareerBliss, Kununu or Zippia, so management style, turnover, pay practices and culture cannot be checked from outside sources. [1] [2]
  • Per the posting summary, the pay range is wide ($35K-$85K) while the posting asks for 10+ years of acquisition marketing experience. The low end of that range is below market for that level of experience. [1]
  • The posting summary lists in-person work only, evenings as needed and a required driver's license. That points to site visits and after-hours work with no remote option. [1]
  • It is a small, owner-run firm (two LLC members on BuildZoom). The marketing role is probably a one-person team with a limited budget, little peer support and no clear promotion path. This is an inference from company size, not a stated fact. [1]
  • Business listings are inconsistent. They give two Gilbert addresses (19356 S Higley Rd and 13823 S 155th St), BuildZoom and Levelset list Queen Creek, Facebook mentions Scottsdale, and Levelset shows 0 projects. Cleaning up these listings would land on the new hire. [1] [2] [3] [4]
  • Not all customer feedback is positive. One 2021 Chamber of Commerce reviewer described a missed appointment and a slow estimate, and Yellow Pages shows a mixed spread of ratings. [1] [2]
  • The local market is crowded and the brand name is shared with others. Houzz shows hundreds of pool pros in the area, with Shasta, Dolphin, Cal Pool and Presidential all targeting Gilbert. 'Pool Envy' (Oklahoma, with BBB complaints) and other 'Envy' brands clutter search results, which makes brand search and reputation management harder. [1] [2] [3] [4]

Watch for

  • Per the posting summary, the pay floor of $35K sits next to a 10+ years experience requirement. Ask where in the range the offer will land before investing more time.
  • I could not confirm the 2026 Pinnacle Award claim. The Luxury Pools + Outdoor Living Pinnacle pages I found (2019-2025) did not show Envy Pools in the results, and no 2026 winners page appeared. Ask for the award announcement, since the marketing role would be promoting it.
  • With no employee reviews anywhere, the interview is the only way to learn about turnover, who held the role before, and why it is open.
  • The posting says 'evenings as needed' and requires in-person work. Get the expected weekly hours and event or site-visit load in writing.
  • Do not mix up Envy Pools with 'Pool Envy' (Edmond, OK, with BBB complaints), 'ENVY' (2.6 on Glassdoor) or Envy Sports Club & Pools (NJ). They are different companies.

Bottom line

There is not enough employee evidence to judge what it is like to work at Envy Pools. All the signals come from customer reviews (mostly positive, small sample), license and permit records (legitimate), and the company's own site. For a marketer the product is visual and the role covers a lot, but this is a small, owner-run firm. Expect a one-person marketing function, in-person work with some evenings, and a pay range that starts low for the 10+ years of experience asked. The Pinnacle Award claim could not be confirmed. Before accepting, Joshua should ask for: the salary figure within $35K-$85K; the marketing budget and any agency support; who he reports to (probably an owner); how success is measured (leads, cost per lead, booked designs); what happened to the last person in the role; and the award documentation.
