## Plain-English summary

You own Envy's marketing end to end: strategy, content, channels, and the results. Their framing is blunt — this is not a posting job. One day you are on an active build with a camera, the next you are cutting a reveal, planning a campaign, reviewing ad performance, or sitting across from a builder who should be sending you projects.

Three distinct jobs in one:

1. **Make the content.** Reels, TikToks, YouTube Shorts, long-form project films, captions and copy. Shot on active construction sites in Arizona heat, early mornings and dusk.
2. **Run the marketing.** Paid social, funnels, lead tracking, analytics. Success is measured by qualified leads, ROAS, and signed revenue, not post count.
3. **Bring in business face to face.** Build referral relationships with custom home builders, architects, designers, and realtors. Represent Envy at industry events.
