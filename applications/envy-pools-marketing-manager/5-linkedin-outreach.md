# LinkedIn Outreach — Marketing Manager, Envy Pools

Envy Pools is a Gilbert company founded in 2018. It is small, so the people who matter are
few and findable. Search LinkedIn for **"Envy Pools"**, filter by People, narrow to the
Phoenix metro. Also check **@envypoolsaz** on Instagram — small companies tag their own
staff, and that is often faster than LinkedIn for finding who runs what.

## Who to target

1. **The owner or a co-founder.** At a company this size, the owner is almost certainly the
   hiring manager for a first real marketing hire. This is the highest-value contact and the
   most reachable.
2. **General Manager or Director of Operations.** The person who will have to work alongside
   you on job sites, and who will have an opinion about whether a marketer can handle a
   build.
3. **Whoever currently posts the content.** There may be an office coordinator or a
   part-timer running the Instagram now. They know exactly what the pain is, and they are
   the easiest person to have a real conversation with.

## Messages

**To the owner or founder**

> Hi [Name] — I just applied for the Marketing Manager role. Quick version of why: I have
> spent seven years filming construction and trades on active job sites across the Valley,
> and I run the marketing behind the footage too. A campaign I led raised ad click-through
> by over 200%, and I built my own company on referral relationships with builders and
> realtors. I am in Mesa, twenty minutes from your office. Construction work here:
> lightbox-digital.com/construction-trades-industrial. Happy to come walk a job site with
> you.

**To the GM or operations lead**

> Hi [Name] — I applied for the Marketing Manager opening and wanted to reach the
> operations side, since the posting makes clear this role lives on active builds. I have
> filmed construction and industrial work in Arizona summers for seven years, hold a Part
> 107 for aerials, and I am used to staying out of a crew's way while getting the shot.
> Work here: lightbox-digital.com/construction-trades-industrial. What does a typical build
> timeline look like for you?

**To whoever runs the content now**

> Hi [Name] — I just applied for the Marketing Manager role and wanted to say hello to the
> person actually doing the work. I shoot, edit, grade, and run the paid side myself, so I
> am mostly curious what the biggest bottleneck is right now — getting on site, turnaround,
> or something else. Portfolio: lightbox-digital.com. Either way, the recent project
> content looks good.

## Notes

- **Lead with construction every time.** Not pools, not video generally. Their stated
  requirement is comfort on active job sites in Arizona heat, and that is the one thing a
  typical marketing applicant cannot claim. It is your clearest differentiator.
- **Offer to come on site.** It costs you a morning and it is exactly the behavior the job
  is asking for. A company that wants someone to go meet builders face to face will notice
  a candidate who shows up.
- **One proof point per message.** The 200% click-through lift is the strongest single
  number for a marketing lead; the construction footage is the strongest asset for an
  operations lead.
- **Ask the pay band early,** given the $65k–$90k vs $35k–$85k discrepancy between the
  posting body and the Indeed field. The owner is the right person to ask, directly and
  without apology.
- Do not send all three the same day. Owner first.
