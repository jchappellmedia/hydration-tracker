Camera

RED and Canon cinema bodies daily at the Arizona State Legislature. Sony FX3 and FX6 across 200+ delivered pieces at Grand Canyon Education. DJI Mavic 3 and gimbal work on over 150 shoots.

Light & grade

Aputure systems — 1200d, 600d, 300d, sky panels, Fresnels. Grading in DaVinci Resolve across S-Log3, Canon Log-3, and RED RGB Wide Gamut; rebuilt one grading pipeline to run 3× faster.

Generation

A working AI pipeline rather than one-off prompts — Runway, Kling, Midjourney, Veo, ElevenLabs, plus Claude agentic coding for the production layer. Cut content production time in half at Charter One and 30% at my own shop.

Broadcast delivery

An alumni campaign that ran during NCAA basketball broadcasts. 200+ pieces delivered to TV, paid social, and organic, reaching millions. Comfortable hitting spec on a deadline.
