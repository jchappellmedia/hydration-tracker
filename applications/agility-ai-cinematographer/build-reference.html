Company research

Agility (agilityads.com) is a small U.S. ad tech company based in Lehi, Utah (some directories list Draper, Utah). It sells what CEO Matt Behrend calls a "brand advertising operating system", one platform covering CTV, display, native, audio, mobile and digital out-of-home ads. Basic facts differ between sources. The founding year is listed as anywhere from 2014 to 2018, headcount as 51-100 or 51-200, and total funding as $3.7M (one profile) or $15.7M (CB Insights). In 2024 it picked Clinch as its main ad-serving and dynamic creative partner. A September 2025 TechBuzz profile describes the company leaning into generative AI. The company's Creative Director posting says its Performance Creative team made 1,100+ creatives for 15+ active brands last year. The AI Video Producer/Cinematographer posting (jobright.ai) describes producing AI-generated video ads for several brands from briefs, scripts and shot lists. It asks for 3+ years of video production and a reel, names tools such as Claude and Higgsfield, and says the hire will help build the AI video practice from the ground up. The posting is tagged "United States" and gives no salary and no clear remote policy. That matters for an applicant in Mesa, AZ. All employee feedback found is company-wide, mostly from 2025, and small in number. None of it is specific to the creative or video team. The searches covered Glassdoor, Indeed, CareerBliss/Comparably/Kununu, news, and layoffs/lawsuit/culture 2026. The Indeed connector failed to connect, so no Indeed company data was pulled.

What employees say: pros and cons

Pros

  • Glassdoor rating is high for a small company: 4.5 of 5 from 33 reviews, with 85% recommending it to a friend. The sample is small and company-wide, mostly from 2025. [1] [2]
  • The most common praise is for leadership. Reviewers say leaders care about people's individual goals and change things based on feedback. One review is titled 'Great culture and leadership you can trust.' [1] [2]
  • Reviewers describe coworkers as talented, supportive and collaborative. [1] [2]
  • Startup setting with room to make an impact. One review calls it a 'start up vibe, performance based', and a Lehi reviewer cites the chance to make an impact on digital advertising. The South Jordan filter has only 2 reviews. [1] [2]
  • The company backs AI-driven creative. The AI Video Producer/Cinematographer role helps build an AI video practice from the ground up with tools like Claude and Higgsfield. The Creative Director posting asks for a forward-leaning attitude toward AI. The CEO talks about generative AI in public. [1] [2] [3]
  • Steady creative volume across many brands. The Performance Creative team made 1,100+ creatives for 15+ active brands last year, covering display, social, video, audio and DOOH. That suggests a varied workload for a video role. [1]
  • Pay and benefits rate well on Glassdoor: 4.4 of 5 for compensation and benefits, with 30 salaries reported for 27 Utah jobs. One reviewer praises the residual-based pay structure, which likely applies to sales roles. [1] [2]

Cons

  • Layoffs: a Glassdoor reviewer says the company laid off some employees during a 'major pivot' and calls it necessary but painful. [1]
  • Some former employees criticize leadership. They cite unclear direction, feedback that never came when requested, and no recognition. One is quoted in search snippets saying 'Shady leadership and the product isn't real.' [1] [2]
  • The work is described as demanding, with heavy coordination between departments and focus that changes as the product develops. [1] [2]
  • Doubts about execution: a reviewer says the company has potential but still needs to figure out how to execute. The one CareerBliss review (2.8, a former VP of Sales, undated) says it lacks direction and runs a 30-person company like a 5-person one. [1] [2]
  • Small, early company with inconsistent public data. Founding year (2014-2018), HQ (Draper vs Lehi), headcount and funding ($3.7M vs $15.7M) all differ between sources, so stability is hard to judge from outside. [1] [2] [3]
  • The job listing shows no salary and no clear remote policy. It is tagged 'United States', and the company is based in Utah while the applicant is in Mesa, AZ. [1]
  • The Glassdoor compensation and benefits rating fell 2% over the last 12 months. [1]

Watch for

  • A Glassdoor reviewer reports layoffs during a 'major pivot' (2025-era reviews). Ask what changed, which teams were cut, and how much runway the company has.
  • One former employee review (quoted in search snippets) says 'Shady leadership and the product isn't real.' It is a single review, but it is a strong claim.
  • The posting shows no salary and no clear remote or on-site policy, and the company is in Utah while the applicant is in Mesa, AZ. Confirm both before investing time.
  • No employee reviews from 2026 and none from creative or video staff were found. The evidence is company-wide, small (33 Glassdoor reviews) and mostly from 2025.
  • Many unrelated employers share the name (Agility logistics E39227, Agility PR Solutions, Agility Recovery, Agility Robotics). Their reviews, including layoffs, the DSV acquisition and 'below market salary' comments, do not apply to agilityads.com.
  • The AI video practice is new ('from the ground up'). Expect to define processes yourself, with little existing structure or senior video mentorship.

Bottom line

Agility (agilityads.com) is a small Lehi, Utah ad tech startup. Its Glassdoor rating is good (4.5 from 33 reviews) but the sample is small, company-wide and mostly from 2025, and none of the reviews come from the creative or video team. Current staff praise leadership that listens and a talented team. Former staff point to layoffs during a pivot, demanding cross-team work, changing priorities and doubts about execution. For an AI Cinematographer, the main draw is a chance to shape a new AI video practice at a company that backs generative AI and produces creative for 15+ brands. The trade-off is startup instability and little existing structure. Before going further, confirm pay (none listed), whether the role can be remote from Mesa, AZ, who the role reports to, what the recent pivot and layoffs changed, and how the company is funded now.
