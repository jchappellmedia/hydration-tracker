Guild Mortgage Company, closing loans and opening doors since 1960. As a mortgage banking firm we are dedicated to serving the home owner/buyer. Our goal is to provide affordable home financing for our customers, utilizing the best terms available while providing a level of professionalism and service unsurpassed in the lending industry.

Position Summary

The AI Training & Enablement Specialist owns an assigned function within the organization's AI training and enablement work, for example training, departmental enablement, communications, adoption, or an emerging program or platform. Working under general direction, the Specialist independently assesses needs within that function, designs and builds the enablement solutions and learning experiences it requires, creates original content, and evaluates and improves effectiveness over time. The role requires the consistent exercise of independent judgment: translating technical AI capabilities for the intended audience, selecting the approach and format, and deciding how the work is structured and measured. The Specialist applies responsible AI, data privacy, and compliance standards to everything the role designs and delivers.

Compensation

This role is an exempt position with a Targeted Salary Range of 82,506 to $118,345 annually.

Compensation at Guild is influenced by a wide array of factors including but not limited to local and federal minimum wage requirements, education, level of experience, and applicant's geographical location.

Essential Functions

  • Design, develop, and maintain the enablement programs, learning journeys, and resources for the assigned function, tailored to different business units and skill levels.
  • Translate technical AI capabilities into practical enablement by partnering with AI Engineers, Data Scientists, Product, and IT, using independent judgment to decide what to convey and how.
  • Design and facilitate workshops, webinars, hands-on labs, office hours, and other sessions, adapting content and delivery based on audience needs and feedback.
  • Assess needs across teams and determine the priorities, gaps, and approach needed to close them, aligned with business goals and AI maturity levels.
  • Create original content and resources, such as playbooks, FAQs, job aids, guides, tutorials, communications, and assessments, exercising professional judgment over structure, format, and modality.
  • Build and maintain the knowledge base for the assigned function, keeping content accurate, current, and easy to find.
  • Incorporate responsible AI, data-handling, and compliance standards into the work, using judgment to tailor guidance to each audience.
  • Stay current on new AI tools and features being introduced across the organization and determine what updates the assigned function needs as a result.
  • Analyze AI usage trends and audience feedback to identify what is working, diagnose adoption barriers, and improve effectiveness.
  • Deliver the enablement components of AI rollouts, including readiness, reinforcement, and launch materials.
  • Define and track measures of effectiveness (adoption, proficiency, satisfaction, question themes) and use the findings to improve the work.
  • Stay current with AI trends, technologies, and enablement best practices to keep the work relevant and effective.
  • Perform other duties as assigned.

Company research

Guild Mortgage is a retail mortgage lender based in San Diego, with branches in 49 states and DC and roughly 4,000 to 5,300 employees (estimates differ by source). In December 2025 Bayview Asset Management closed a $1.3B all-cash deal that took Guild private and delisted it from the NYSE. The executive team and brand stayed, and the CEO told staff the change should feel like a "non-event." Guild built an in-house AI assistant, GuildGPT, in 2024. It is now hiring for AI enablement, AI governance, and AI engineering roles. The AI Training & Enablement Specialist role is listed as remote in the US (req AITRA018532, posted Sept 29, 2026). It builds AI workshops, labs, playbooks, and knowledge bases, and works with the AI, data, product, and IT teams. Posted pay is $82,506 to $118,345 on Edtech.com, while Built In shows a flat $118K. Employee reviews are mixed (Glassdoor 3.5, Indeed 3.4). Nearly all of them come from loan officers, processors, and branch staff. None are specific to training, L&D, AI, or marketing/creative teams.

What employees say: pros and cons

Pros

  • The company is investing in AI, so this role sits in a function that is growing. Guild built GuildGPT in-house; by July 2024 nearly 2,000 employees had used it and it had answered over 20,000 questions. Guild is also hiring a Lead, AI Governance (about $109K-$157K) and AI Engineers. [1] [2] [3] [4]
  • The role is remote in the US, and Guild posts other US-remote corporate roles (legal, data engineering, product). One posting says non-California candidates can be fully remote. [1] [2] [3] [4]
  • Employees praise the training and onboarding. A 2026 Indeed review calls it 'incredible,' and others cite coaching and self-help training resources. This suggests the company values training, which fits an enablement role. [1] [2]
  • Coworkers and team culture get good marks. CareerBliss reviewers list culture, people, and managers as pros (4.1 average, but only 13 ratings). Comparably employees are most satisfied with team, environment, and meetings. One Glassdoor review title reads 'Good teammates and overall company culture.' [1] [2] [3]
  • Leadership stayed in place after the Bayview buyout. The executive team and the brand were kept, the CEO told staff the deal should feel like a 'non-event,' and no layoffs tied to the deal have been reported. [1] [2] [3]
  • The pay range is posted ($82,506 to $118,345, exempt, full-time), and the overall Glassdoor rating (3.5) is in line with the finance industry average. [1] [2]

Cons

  • Overwork is a common complaint: mandated overtime several times a week, after-hours meetings, many meetings, and some Indeed reports of 70-hour weeks. Glassdoor work-life balance fell 6% over the past year. [1] [2] [3] [4]
  • Management draws the most criticism. About 30 Glassdoor reviews cite poor management or unprofessional behavior, and reviewers say managers don't take feedback and can't stick with a decision. Comparably's sample (about 40) gives a net promoter score of 52% detractors and 29% promoters. [1] [2] [3]
  • Pay and raises are weak spots. Reviewers report raises of 2-4% and late notice on when raises take effect, and 8 reviews say salaries are not competitive. PayScale rates fair pay at 2.4 out of 5, and CareerBliss reviewers say pay is slightly below industry standard. [1] [2] [3]
  • Layoffs follow the mortgage cycle. In 2022 Guild cut staff to save about $40M a year. About 200 people were cut in the Academy Mortgage acquisition, mostly back-office staff, and reviewers say Academy's corporate staff, including IT, were let go. Reviewers describe layoffs that 'happen in waves.' [1] [2] [3] [4]
  • Reviewers describe the technology as antiquated and proprietary and the company as disorganized. This could make driving AI adoption harder for an enablement specialist. [1] [2]
  • Ratings are trending down. In the September 2026 Glassdoor snapshot, culture and values fell 7% over the past year, and the recommend-to-a-friend rate ranges from 61% to 68% across snapshots. [1] [2]

Watch for

  • New owner: Bayview took Guild private in December 2025. No layoffs have been reported since, but budgets and priorities for newer functions such as AI enablement could change under the new owner. Ask how the AI program is funded and who sponsors it.
  • Layoff history: about $40M a year in cuts in 2022, and about 200 cut in the Academy acquisition, mostly back office including IT. Corporate support roles have been cut before.
  • Remote policy is not confirmed. Flex Index lists a 3-day corporate office rule, but that page says its content is AI-generated and unverified. Get remote status for Arizona in writing.
  • Pay listings disagree: Edtech.com shows $82,506-$118,345, while Built In shows a flat $118K. Confirm the actual band and where you would land in it.
  • Litigation with former staff: Guild won a $10.7M arbitration award against three ex-employees who moved to CrossCountry, a former Northeast division VP sued over $750K in unpaid compensation and wrongful termination, and Guild paid an older ~$25M FHA False Claims Act settlement. Read any non-solicit or non-compete terms in the offer.
  • Small or old samples: Blind has 1 review (2021), CareerBliss has 13 ratings, and Comparably has about 40-50. No Reddit threads were found. No reviews are specific to training, AI, or marketing/creative teams, so the company-wide ratings come from sales and operations staff.
  • The Indeed MCP tool failed to connect, so Indeed figures come from search-result snapshots only.

Bottom line

Guild is a middle-of-the-road employer (Glassdoor 3.5, Indeed 3.4). Most reviews come from loan officers and processors, not corporate training or AI staff, so they say little about this team. The case for the role: it is remote in the US, Guild is building a real AI function (GuildGPT, AI governance, AI engineering), employees value training, and the pay range is posted. The risks: overwork, criticism of management, small raises, layoffs that follow the mortgage cycle, culture scores dropping over the past year, and a new owner since December 2025. Questions to ask in interviews: Who leads the AI program and how big is the team? How did the Bayview deal affect budget and headcount for AI work? Is the role fully remote from Arizona, in writing? What is the raise cycle? What happened to corporate roles during past downturns?
