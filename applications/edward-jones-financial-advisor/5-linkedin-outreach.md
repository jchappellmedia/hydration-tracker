# LinkedIn Outreach Strategy — Edward Jones

Edward Jones hires advisors through regional recruiters and existing advisors who sponsor candidates. Outreach genuinely moves the needle here — advisor referrals get fast-tracked.

## Who to target
1. **An Edward Jones Financial Advisor in the East Valley (Mesa/Gilbert/Queen Creek).** Search LinkedIn for "Edward Jones Financial Advisor Mesa AZ." Local advisors know which offices are open and can refer you — referrals carry real weight in their process.
2. **Edward Jones Talent Acquisition / Financial Advisor Recruiter (Arizona or Southwest region).** Search "Edward Jones recruiter financial advisor." These recruiters own the hiring pipeline for new advisors.
3. **A Branch Office Administrator (BOA) you may know locally** — they hear about advisor openings first.

## Why the advisor coffee chat matters
Edward Jones screens hard for "do you understand what this job really is." Talking to a working advisor before your interview gives you honest expectations and a referral, and interviewers can tell when a candidate has done this homework.

## Tone reminders
- Respectful and brief — advisors get pitched constantly.
- Ask about *their* practice; don't pitch yourself hard in message one.
- Follow up once after a week if no reply, then let it go.
