# LinkedIn Outreach — Turning Point Action

Field programs hire fast and hire on referral. A direct message often beats an online form here, because the people staffing a territory are usually the ones reading applications.

Search LinkedIn for **"Turning Point Action"**, filter by People, then narrow by location to Arizona — the organization is headquartered in Phoenix, so local staff are findable.

## Who to target

1. **Ballot Chase Manager / Regional Field Director** — the posting says you report to this person daily. They own the hiring for their own territory.
2. **Field Director / Director of Grassroots** — one level up, useful if the local manager does not respond.
3. **Recruiter / Talent Acquisition** — fastest route to the actual listing, the zip code, and the pay.

> I cannot browse LinkedIn from here. Send me names and titles and I will tailor each message.

## Messages

**To a Ballot Chase Manager or Field Director**

> Hi [Name] — I am applying for a Ballot Chaser role and wanted to reach out. I ran my own business for six years, so working a territory alone against a weekly number is familiar. I generated my own leads, tracked goals in spreadsheets every week, and held 5.0-star reviews for over a year. Which territories are you staffing?

**To a recruiter**

> Hi [Name] — I am interested in the Ballot Chaser opening. Before I apply, can you tell me the zip code on the posting and the pay structure? I am in Mesa and want to confirm I am inside the three-mile radius. Happy to send my resume either way.

## Notes

- **The recruiter message does double duty.** It gets you in front of a person and answers the two open questions in one move. Send that one first.
- Ask about the zip code before you spend effort on anything else. If you are outside three miles, the rest is moot.
- One proof point per message. The weekly goal tracking is the most relevant one — it is the daily reporting this job is built on.
- Keep it short. Field staff read messages on their phones between doors.
