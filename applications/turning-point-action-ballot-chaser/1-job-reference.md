## Plain-English summary

This is field work on the "Chase The Vote" program. A ballot chaser works a defined territory, identifies voters in that territory who do not vote often, builds relationships in those neighborhoods, and encourages people to return their ballots. You report to a Ballot Chase Manager every day, track your activity in spreadsheets, and work against incentivized targets.

It is self-directed, in-person, goal-driven work. You are in a car and on doorsteps, not at a desk.
