## Plain-English Summary
Vertex provides back-office and support services — academics, finance, technology, HR, communications, marketing, facilities, construction, food service — to schools and education organizations. This role owns the marketing engine that brings in new school clients.

It is a **B2B demand-generation job**. The Director sets pipeline targets, runs paid and organic channels, owns the website and content, runs conferences and events, manages agencies and budgets, and works alongside a Partnerships team on shared revenue goals. They stress it is "not a role for a distant strategist" — hands-on execution is expected.

## Main Areas
1. **Demand generation & ROI** — own strategy across paid, organic, referral, event, partnership. Set MQL, pipeline, and acquisition-efficiency targets. Manage budgets and agencies.
2. **Partnerships alignment** — define lead qualification and routing, own funnel visibility, build sales enablement (case studies, customer stories, presentations).
3. **Brand, content, organic** — brand positioning, website strategy, content and SEO, thought leadership, webinars.
4. **Conferences & events** — own event and sponsorship strategy with ROI measures; position leaders for speaking opportunities.
5. **Campaigns** — integrated campaigns, disciplined testing, data-driven course correction, repeatable playbooks.
6. **Marketing ops & team leadership** — operating system, team development, CRM/automation/analytics stack, annual budget, PR and crisis communications.

## Required
- 7+ years progressive **B2B** marketing owning demand generation, customer acquisition, and pipeline
- Proven ownership of marketing performance and ROI, including budget reallocation by channel
- Strong command of B2B revenue funnels (MQLs, nurturing, conversion, attribution); shared pipeline with sales/BD
- Strong analytical skills with marketing, CRM, and pipeline data
- Marketing operations and project management; events, sponsorships, agencies, vendors
- Experience leading marketing teams; bachelor's or graduate degree in Marketing, Business, or related

## Preferred
- Marketing B2B services to **schools, school systems, education organizations**, government, or complex institutional buyers
- Multi-service or professional-services organization with relationship-driven sales cycles
- SEO, content strategy, thought leadership, conversion optimization
- Conference, association, sponsorship, field marketing; sales enablement and case studies
- **HubSpot** CRM and marketing automation ownership; senior PR or crisis communications
