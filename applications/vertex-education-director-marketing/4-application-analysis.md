# Application Analysis & Feedback — Director of Marketing, Vertex Education

## Job Match: Partial — and I want to correct what I told you

When I surfaced this job I called it a strong lead based on the sector, pay, and commute. Reading the full posting, that was too optimistic and the correction matters because it changes how you should spend your effort.

This is a **B2B demand-generation role**. Vertex sells services *to* schools and school systems. The spine of the job is pipeline: marketing-qualified leads, nurturing, attribution, CRM, and shared revenue targets with a Partnerships team. Your marketing record is consumer-facing — you market schools *to parents*. Those are different disciplines that happen to share a vocabulary.

Fit is **Partial**, and the gap sits on the required list rather than the preferred one.

## Where you are genuinely strong

- **You know the buyer better than almost any applicant will.** Vertex sells to school leaders. You sit with charter school executives now, taught in a charter classroom, and produced for one of the largest education marketing agencies in the country. Their top *preferred* qualification is "marketing B2B services to schools, school systems, education organizations." You have lived on the customer side of that exact transaction.
- **Education results with real numbers** — 2x enrollment for a charter school, 200% CTR lift, 20x engagement, 20% enrollment increase at ALA.
- **Degree requirement exceeded** — Master's in Marketing with a Marketing Analytics specialization, plus MBA coursework.
- **Content, customer stories, and case studies** — a listed responsibility and the thing you do every week. Their sales enablement needs are your day job.
- **Budgets, vendors, and teams** — Lightbox finances, $1M in gear at GCE, $30k inventory at ALA, directing teams of 5–20.
- **HubSpot Inbound certified** — they name HubSpot under preferred.
- **Fingerprint clearance** — the role requires an IVP Level One card. As a former ALA teacher you have likely held one, which removes a real onboarding hurdle. Mention it if true.

## Where the gap is real

| Required | Your position |
|---|---|
| 7+ years progressive **B2B** marketing owning demand gen and pipeline | ~6 years marketing, consumer-facing. No B2B demand gen. |
| B2B revenue funnels — MQLs, nurturing, conversion, attribution | Not documented |
| Building shared pipeline with sales/business development | Not documented |
| CRM and marketing automation ownership (HubSpot admin) | Certification, not platform ownership |
| Leading a **marketing team** with roles and performance management | Led production teams, not a marketing function |
| Marketing ROI ownership, reallocating budget by channel results | Partial — you set revenue goals and tracked spend for your own business |

None of that is on the resume, and none of it should be. A Director role gets a real interview screen, and claiming pipeline experience you cannot discuss for ten minutes would end badly.

## How to play it

Apply — the education-buyer insight is rare and it is exactly what they say they want. But go in knowing you are the "adjacent and hungry" candidate, not the safe hire, and that they may want someone with a B2B pipeline track record.

Two things strengthen the application:

1. **Say the quiet part first.** In an interview: "My marketing has been consumer-facing — schools to families. What I bring that a B2B marketer usually cannot is knowing how school leaders actually evaluate and buy services, because I sit with them." That reframes the gap as a trade rather than a deficit.
2. **Learn the vocabulary before you interview.** HubSpot Academy has free courses on lifecycle stages, lead scoring, and attribution — the same academy that issued your Inbound cert. A weekend closes enough of the language gap to hold the conversation: https://academy.hubspot.com/

## Bottom line
Worth an application because the sector knowledge is genuinely differentiating and the pay band ($81k–$125k) sits above your floor with room to negotiate. Just do not let it crowd out the roles where you are the obvious hire — **Valleywise** and the **Achieve Education Group** Marketing Manager job are both closer to your documented record.
