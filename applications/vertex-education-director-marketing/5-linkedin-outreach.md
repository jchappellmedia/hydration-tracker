# LinkedIn Outreach Strategy — Vertex Education

Outreach matters more than usual here. You are the adjacent candidate on paper, so a human who understands the education-buyer angle is worth more than a clean resume screen.

## Who to target
Search LinkedIn for **"Vertex Education"**, filter by People:
1. **VP of Marketing / Chief Growth Officer** — likely the hiring manager for a Director of Marketing.
2. **Head of Partnerships or VP of Partnerships** — this role partners with them daily, and they care most about someone who understands how school leaders buy. Your strongest listener.
3. **Talent Acquisition / HR Business Partner** at Vertex.
4. **Anyone you already know at Charter One, Leona Group, ALA, or GCE who has worked with Vertex** — a warm intro from inside the charter world beats every cold message here.

That fourth one is worth ten minutes of thought before you send anything cold. The Arizona charter network is small, and you have been in it for seven years.

## Messages

**To the VP of Partnerships**
> Subject: Director of Marketing — applied
>
> Hi [Name], I applied for the Director of Marketing role. Most marketing candidates will not have sat on the school side of the table — I have, for seven years. I plan enrollment strategy with charter executives now, taught in a charter classroom, and helped a school double enrollment in a year. I would bring that buyer perspective to how Vertex markets to school leaders. Would welcome a conversation.

**To the hiring manager / VP of Marketing**
> Subject: Director of Marketing application
>
> Hi [Name], I submitted my application for Director of Marketing. Quick summary: Master's in Marketing Analytics, seven years inside education, and campaigns with measured results — 200% lift in ad click-through, 20x engagement, 2x enrollment for a charter school. I am in Mesa and would enjoy talking about how Vertex reaches school leaders.

**To a recruiter**
> Subject: Applied — Director of Marketing, Chandler
>
> Hi [Name], I applied for the Director of Marketing position in Chandler. I bring a Master's in Marketing Analytics and seven years working inside schools and education organizations, which lines up with the institutional-buyer experience listed as preferred. Happy to answer anything.

## Tone
Vertex's language is mission-forward — "be the one who makes a difference." Match the sincerity without copying the slogans. Calm, specific, education-fluent.

## One thing to prepare
If asked about B2B demand generation, do not deflect. Name it and trade: consumer-side marketing experience, but rare insight into how the buyer decides. That answer is stronger than a vague claim of pipeline work.
