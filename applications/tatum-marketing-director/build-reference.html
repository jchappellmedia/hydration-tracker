Job Description Reference — Marketing Director, The Officer Tatum

Application Details

  • Company: Tatum Management Group LLC (The Officer Tatum LLC)
  • Role: Marketing Director
  • Location: Scottsdale, AZ — Remote
  • Type: Full-time
  • Pay: $75,000 – $100,000 per year + performance bonus up to 20%
  • Posted: ~11 days before July 18, 2026 (via ZipRecruiter)
  • Benefits: Health insurance, PTO, holidays, retirement plan, professional development allowance

Company Info

The Officer Tatum LLC is a fast-growing media and e-commerce company. It runs a portfolio of brands including TatumStore.com, ShinergyLife.com, and PureChoiceApparel.com. The company partners with high-profile influencers and clients on campaigns, events, and content that reach millions of people each month. Its stated mission: combine innovation, performance-driven marketing, and cultural influence to inspire and engage communities nationwide.

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop and oversee multi-channel campaigns (email, paid ads, social, video, influencer collaborations) for TatumStore.com, ShinergyLife.com, PureChoiceApparel.com, and client brands.
  • Build and manage a comprehensive email marketing program: segmentation, automation, performance optimization.
  • Lead paid advertising across Google Ads, YouTube, Meta, TikTok, and emerging platforms.
  • Collaborate with design, content, and video teams on materials for merch drops, interviews, podcasts, and live events.
  • Drive brand awareness for The Officer Tatum's media presence (appearances, speaking engagements, partnerships).
  • Support marketing strategy and execution for clients and influencers in the network.
  • Establish and track KPIs, dashboards, and reporting frameworks.
  • Conduct market and audience research.
  • Oversee brand consistency across all campaigns.
  • Manage external vendors, agencies, and freelancers.

Qualifications

  • 5+ years progressive marketing experience; 3+ years in a leadership role.
  • Track record building and scaling campaigns for e-commerce, consumer brands, or digital media.
  • Strong paid media expertise (Google Ads, Meta Ads, YouTube, TikTok).
  • Success with email platforms (Klaviyo, Mailchimp, or similar).
  • Creative campaign development for merch, events, or influencer content.
  • Analytics tools: Google Analytics, Meta Business Manager, Looker, etc.
  • Strong leadership, communication, and project management.
  • Entrepreneurial and comfortable in a fast-paced environment.

Compensation Notes

  • Bonus up to 20% tied to ROI, customer growth, event success, and client satisfaction.
  • Professional development allowance for courses, conferences, certifications.

Company research

What employees say: pros and cons

Pros

  • Fully remote, full-time role with a posted pay range of $75k-$100k, a performance bonus of up to 20%, and benefits (medical, PTO, retirement; the saved posting also lists a professional development allowance). [1] [2]
  • Large existing audience to market to: the founder's YouTube channel has about 3.7M subscribers and he hosts a national radio show distributed by Salem Radio Network. The marketing lead would not be starting from zero. [1] [2]
  • Several brands in one job (merch store, wellness/apparel e-commerce, media, client and influencer work), which gives broad, varied marketing experience. [1] [2]
  • Small, founder-led company (LinkedIn lists 11-50 employees). A Marketing Director would likely work directly with the founder and own marketing end to end. [1] [2]
  • The business has run since late 2019 and has grown from a YouTube channel into radio, books and stores, so it is not a brand-new startup. [1] [2]

Cons

  • No employee reviews exist on Glassdoor, Indeed or Reddit, so there is no outside view of management, pay fairness, hours or turnover. The 'Tatum' ratings that show up belong to a different company. [1] [2]
  • The BBB gives The Officer Tatum Store an F, partly for not responding to 3 complaints. Customer reviews average 2.25/5 and mention unshipped orders and unanswered emails. These are customer complaints, not employee reviews, and the sample is small, but a Marketing Director would inherit that reputation problem. [1] [2]
  • The brand is tied to a politically polarizing commentator who draws public criticism. YouTube videos also make unconfirmed claims about lawsuits involving him. Some future employers may view the work as partisan. [1] [2] [3]
  • The team is probably thin: LinkedIn says 11-50 employees but Buzzfile estimates 1 at the listed location. The director may do much of the hands-on work themselves, with little support staff or HR structure. [1] [2]
  • The role has been posted since early July 2026 and keeps getting re-posted. On 2026-10-14 ZipRecruiter showed a fresh 9-day-old listing, and an earlier copy was marked as re-posted. Open for 3+ months can mean a high bar, a hard-to-fill role, or turnover. Ask why. [1]
  • The employer name differs between postings: Tatum Management Group LLC on ZipRecruiter and Talent.com, The Officer Tatum LLC on Breezy and Remotive. A search for 'Tatum Management Group LLC' found an unrelated Florida contractor of that name and no Arizona record. Confirm which entity would actually employ and pay him. [1] [2] [3]
  • Part of the pay depends on performance: the bonus of up to 20% is tied to ROI, customer growth, event success and client satisfaction. One aggregator copy lists only $75,000, so the offer may start at the low end of the range. [1] [2]

Watch for

  • No employee reviews anywhere, so culture and management cannot be checked from outside.
  • BBB F rating on the company's store for unanswered complaints (customer-side, small sample).
  • The Marketing Director listing has been open and re-posted since early July 2026.
  • Employer name differs between postings (Tatum Management Group LLC vs The Officer Tatum LLC). Confirm the employing entity.
  • Brand is tied to a politically polarizing public figure, which could affect how future employers read the job.

Bottom line

This is a real company with a real, remote, reasonably paid role, and it has a large built-in audience. But there are no employee reviews, so culture is unknown, and the only outside rating is a BBB F on the store side. It is a small, founder-run, politically branded company, and the role has been open since July. If Joshua goes ahead, he should ask in the interview: how big the team is and who he would report to, what the marketing budget is, how the 20% bonus is measured, which legal entity would employ him, and why the role has stayed open since July.
