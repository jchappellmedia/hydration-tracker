## Plain-English summary

Runs the in-house creative team and all outbound creative across email, website, social, paid media, print, recruiting, and customer communications. Half manager, half maker: you assign and review the team's work, own the intake and scheduling system in Asana, and still do hands-on design and copy for priority campaigns.

The job has three distinct pieces:
1. **Leadership** — manage and mentor designers and creative specialists, assign work by capacity, give art direction and critique.
2. **Operations** — own the Creative Services workflow in Asana: intake, prioritization, timelines, approvals, reporting. Maintain brand standards, templates, and the asset library.
3. **Craft** — hands-on design, copy-editing, campaign development, and performance review across channels.
