Full job description A1 Garage Door Service is the national leader in garage door repair and installation. As we continue to grow, we are expanding our in-house creative and digital communication capabilities to support brand development, marketing campaigns, customer communications, recruiting initiatives, and business growth across our markets.

Reporting to the Head of Content, the Creative Services Manager leads the creative team and oversees digital communications across email, website, social media, paid media, print, and other customer-facing channels. This hands-on role turns brand strategy into clear, consistent creative work and ensures projects are delivered on time and aligned with business objectives.

What You’ll Do

  • Lead day-to-day creative and digital communication execution across marketing campaigns, email, website, social media, paid media, recruiting initiatives, print collateral, customer communications, and other branded assets
  • Manage and mentor designers, creative specialists, and internal contributors, assigning work based on priorities and team capacity
  • Own the Creative Services workflow within Asana, including project intake, prioritization, capacity planning, timelines, resource allocation, approvals, and reporting
  • Oversee digital communication plans, content calendars, and publishing schedules across email, website, and social media
  • Partner with department leaders to prioritize requests and translate business goals into clear creative briefs and communication plans
  • Review and approve copy, design, digital content, and campaign materials for accuracy, quality, and brand consistency
  • Provide art direction and constructive feedback for graphic design, photography, video, and other creative assets
  • Collaborate with marketing, content, recruiting, operations, and field teams to plan and execute campaigns, promotions, announcements, and local market initiatives
  • Monitor performance across digital channels and use campaign, email, website, and social media metrics to recommend improvements
  • Contribute hands-on design, copy-editing, and content development for priority campaigns, presentations, advertisements, sales materials, and branded communications as needed
  • Maintain design systems, templates, brand standards, and the creative asset library
  • Oversee digital platforms, vendors, and files prepared for web, email, print, and production
  • Improve creative workflows, file management, quality assurance, and project delivery
  • Collaborate in person 4-5 days per week with marketing, recruiting, operations, and field teams
  • Adapt quickly to shifting priorities in a fast-paced, high-growth environment

What You’ll Bring

  • 6-8 years of experience in creative services, digital communications, brand design, marketing design, or an agency environment, including multi-channel campaign work
  • 2+ years of experience leading creative projects, managing creative teams, or developing team members
  • Strong portfolio or work samples demonstrating creative leadership, campaign development, and hands-on design execution
  • Experience developing creative concepts and art direction across digital, print, video, advertising, and brand campaigns
  • Strong writing, editing, proofreading, and copy-editing skills with the ability to maintain a consistent brand voice
  • Experience with social media, email marketing, content management systems, digital publishing, and performance reporting
  • Advanced proficiency in Adobe Creative Suite and Asana or a comparable project management platform
  • Strong understanding of brand management, design systems, digital communications, and visual storytelling
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities, keep projects on schedule, and communicate clearly with stakeholders
  • Strong eye for layout, typography, color, and visual hierarchy

Key Attributes for Success

  • Strong leadership skills with the ability to guide, develop, and hold creative teams accountable
  • Excellent communication and collaboration skills across departments and locations
  • Strong organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities, deadlines, and projects
  • Strategic and creative thinking with a focus on brand consistency and business results
  • Adaptability and a process-improvement mindset in a fast-paced, growing organization

Company research

A1 Garage Door Service is a residential garage door repair and installation company. Tommy Mello founded it in 2007, and it is based in Phoenix, Arizona. It finds customers through direct-to-consumer marketing across many channels. A third-party listing says it has 54+ locations in 15+ states. It has bought regional brands, including Ideal Garage Doors, Don's Garage, Garage Door Doctor and Welborn Garage Doors. Cortec Group made a growth investment in December 2022. On September 2, 2026, Reuters reported that KKR had agreed to buy A1 for about $2 billion. I found no closing announcement, so treat the deal as pending or unconfirmed. Great Place To Work lists 1,038 US employees. A data-broker estimate of about 142 employees, with about 7 in marketing, conflicts with that and looks out of date. Most employee reviews on Glassdoor and Indeed cover the whole company and come mainly from technicians, sales staff and call-center workers. Only a few reviews are about marketing, so evidence on the team Joshua would join is thin. The Indeed company-data tool failed to connect, so the Indeed figures below come from search-result snippets.

What employees say: pros and cons

Pros

  • Employees rate the company well overall: 4.7/5 on Glassdoor from 306 reviews (95% would recommend) and about 4.3-4.4/5 on Indeed from about 267-290 reviews. These are company-wide numbers, driven mostly by technicians and sales staff, not marketing. [1] [2]
  • Training is the strength reviewers mention most often, across sites and locations. They cite a paid academy, the tools and support new hires get, and coworkers who answer questions. [1] [2]
  • Great Place To Work certified A1 in February 2026, its first year in the program: 87% of surveyed employees called it a great place to work, against a 57% US average. AZ Big Media also named it one of Arizona's Most Admired Companies of 2026. A third party ran the survey, but A1 chose to take part and announced the result itself. [1] [2]
  • Recent reviews from marketing staff are positive. 2026 Marketing Manager reviews on Indeed describe the work as fast-paced and fun, with room to build new processes. The sample is small. [1]
  • Marketing matters to the business: A1 depends on direct-to-consumer marketing across many channels. A posting found through search says A1 is expanding its in-house creative and digital communications (pay listed at $105K-$110K, exact title not confirmed). A separate posting covers marketing for acquired brands at $125K-$140K. Both point to real creative work and budget. [1] [2] [3]
  • Leadership talks about culture in public and works on it. The founder calls staff 'coworkers' or 'team' and promotes a shared-success philosophy on industry podcasts. Reviewers mention camaraderie and internal promotion. Some of this is company framing (one source is sponsored content). [1] [2] [3]

Cons

  • The marketing department's leadership has drawn specific criticism. A former Area Marketing Project Manager rated A1 2.0 in 2023 and blamed marketing leadership. A Glassdoor con says there is potential in marketing 'but not with current leadership.' This is a small sample and may be out of date, but it is the only evidence about the team itself. [1] [2]
  • Some 2026 Indeed reviews allege favoritism in salary, bonuses and PTO, and one says HR let retaliation reports go unaddressed. Another recent review says management works people hard and offers no job security. [1] [2]
  • Long hours come up again and again: 'Incredibly long hours,' a 40-hour week described as the minimum, long days in Las Vegas. Work-life balance is Indeed's lowest sub-rating (4.2). Most of these reviews come from field roles. [1] [2] [3]
  • The culture is built around sales. Technicians work on commission, raises depend on sales, and one reviewer says customers get sold things they do not need. A creative manager may be judged on lead and sales numbers more than on brand work. [1] [2] [3]
  • Ownership is changing. Cortec Group invested in December 2022, and in September 2026 KKR reportedly agreed to buy A1 for about $2 billion. A new private-equity owner can bring changes in leadership, budgets and structure, including in marketing. I found no confirmation that the deal has closed. [1] [2] [3]
  • Ratings may be slipping, and Phoenix headquarters scores below the company as a whole: 4.5 on Glassdoor in Phoenix from 90 reviews against 4.7 overall. One summary of the search results says 2023-2026 reviews are more mixed than 2020-2022. Reviewers also mention limited parking at the new building. [1] [2] [3]

Watch for

  • Pending sale to KKR (reported Sept 2, 2026, about $2B, closing not confirmed). Ask how the deal affects the marketing org, its budget, reporting lines and the security of this role.
  • Marketing-specific reviews (2023 Indeed review rated 2.0; an undated Glassdoor con) criticize marketing leadership. Ask who leads marketing now and whether that leadership has changed since 2023.
  • Several 2026 Indeed reviews allege favoritism in pay, bonuses and PTO, and one says HR did not act on retaliation reports.
  • A third-party listing says the company is building out in-house creative at $105K-$110K, but I could not confirm whether that is the Creative Services Manager posting. Confirm the pay range directly.
  • Employee counts conflict between sources (about 142 in one data-broker estimate vs 1,038 on Great Place To Work). Use the GPTW figure and ask how large the marketing and creative team is.

Bottom line

For a Phoenix-area employer, A1's numbers are strong: Glassdoor 4.7 from 306 reviews, Indeed about 4.3-4.4, Great Place To Work certified in 2026. Training and team culture get consistent praise, and the company is investing in in-house creative work. But almost all reviews come from technicians and sales staff. The few about marketing are mixed: positive in 2026, but a 2023 review and an undated Glassdoor con fault marketing leadership. Watch three things: a sales-driven culture with long hours, 2026 claims of favoritism, and a pending KKR buyout that could change leadership and budgets. My searches turned up no lawsuits and no WARN layoff notices under A1's name. In interviews, Joshua should ask who leads marketing, how big the creative team is, how creative work is measured (brand work vs lead numbers), what the KKR deal means for the role, and what the pay range is.
