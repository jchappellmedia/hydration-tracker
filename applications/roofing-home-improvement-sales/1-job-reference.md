# Job Description Reference — Roofing & Home Improvement Sales (Phoenix area)

## Application Details
- **Company:** Not named anywhere in the ad. Apply via Indeed with resume, or text "MONDAY" with your name to 480-788-6086.
- **Role:** Door-to-door canvassing and cold calling — roofing, windows, doors, HVAC, exteriors
- **Pay:** 100% commission. Ad claims $100K–$300K/year, "$3,000–$9,000 per week," "$150K is our low end."
- **Location:** On the road (Phoenix metro implied by the 480 number)
- **Requirements:** 18+, reliable transportation (non-negotiable), coachable, committed, thick skin
- **Offer in ad:** A no-commitment one-day shadow ("come spend one day with us on your day off")

## Plain-English Summary
This is door-to-door canvassing for home improvement services, paid on straight commission. You knock neighborhoods (including around active job sites), make cold calls, and follow up. The ad promises training by shadowing top closers from day one, and claims new reps reach $5,000/week within 90 days. No experience or resume required; coachability and work ethic are the pitch.

## What They Expect
- Knock doors and make cold calls daily, in the heat.
- Be teachable and follow the system exactly.
- Full commitment; they cut people quickly who don't produce.
- Reliable transportation.
- Upbeat team culture; celebrate wins loudly.
