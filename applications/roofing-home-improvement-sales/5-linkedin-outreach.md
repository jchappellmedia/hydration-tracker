# Outreach Strategy — Roofing & Home Improvement Sales

LinkedIn outreach mostly doesn't apply here — the company is unnamed and this world runs on texts, not LinkedIn. The ad gives you the direct channel, so use it.

## The play
1. **Text "MONDAY" with your name to 480-788-6086** — that's their fast lane and it signals you follow instructions (which is their #1 screen: "did exactly what we told them to do").
2. When they reply, ask for the **company name** and the **shadow day**. Suggested text after first contact:

> Thanks — I'm in for the one-day challenge. Two quick things: what's the company name so I can look you up, and what day works this week? I've run my own business for six years and I've worked with construction companies before, so this world isn't new to me.

3. Once you have the company name, spend ten minutes verifying: AZ Registrar of Contractors license (roc.az.gov), Google reviews, BBB. Then decide about the shadow day.

## If they have a LinkedIn / owner presence
After learning the name, look up the owner or sales manager. One short message:

> Hi [Name], I texted in about the sales role and I'm coming out for a shadow day. Quick intro: six years running my own company, worked with construction and remodel businesses, and I don't melt in the heat — I raced a Spartan course in 100 degrees. See you out there.

## Tone
Match their energy — short, direct, zero corporate polish. Show up on time, ask the middle-of-the-pack reps real numbers, and keep your own counsel on the hype.
