## Logistics worth weighing

**The commute.** North Phoenix from Mesa, five days a week, 9 to 5. That is roughly 30 to 45 miles each way depending on exactly where their office sits, so realistically 40 to 60 minutes in morning traffic. That is the longest commute of anything in your tracker, and the posting is blunt that remote is not an option. Over a year it is real money and real hours.

Placement is "based on demonstrated qualifications," and with seven-plus years against a preferred four, plus a documented pipeline rebuild, you have a genuine case for the upper half. **The number is yours to give, not mine** — but do not let an offer land at $70k without a conversation.

That second part is the one people fumble. Do not just send the reel. Send three or four finished pieces with one line each on what you personally did:

- **The veterans documentary** — story, edit, color, sound. Your best narrative piece.
