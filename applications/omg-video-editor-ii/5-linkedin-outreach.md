# Outreach — Video Editor II, Offensive Marketing Group

Unusually, the posting names the person: **Richard Mansfield**, richard.mansfield@offensivegroup.com. The email application goes straight to a human, so the usual cold-outreach problem does not apply here.

That changes the strategy. The email **is** the outreach. Make it excellent and you may not need anything else.

## Priority order

1. **The email to Richard Mansfield.** Four required items, done properly. This is the whole application.
2. **LinkedIn follow-up to Richard** a few days later if nothing comes back. Short, no pressure.
3. **Post-Production Supervisor or Creative Director at OMG** if you want a second touch. Search LinkedIn for "Offensive Marketing Group" and filter by People.

## LinkedIn follow-up to Richard (only if no reply after about a week)

> Hi Richard — I sent my resume and reel for the Video Editor II role last week and wanted to make sure it reached you. Short version: seven years carrying client projects alone from ingest to delivery, and I rebuilt a grading pipeline to run 3x faster in Resolve on log and wide-gamut originals. Happy to resend anything.

## Notes

- **Do not send a bare reel.** They said applications without work samples will not be considered, and they asked for your specific contribution on collaborative projects. Send finished pieces with one line each on what you did.
- **Lead with the Resolve pipeline rebuild.** It is your best single line for this specific employer.
- Keep the follow-up light and a week apart. A small agency hiring one editor is not ignoring you; they are watching reels.
- The firearms and outdoor interest paragraph has to be in your own words. Everyone at that agency will be able to tell.
