## Plain-English summary

A senior individual-contributor editing seat. You take a project from ingest to client-ready delivery on your own: organize and transcode media, read the brief, find the story, cut it, grade it, mix it, build the motion graphics, QC it, and export to spec. Minimal supervision. They are explicit that this is not entry level and not short-form assembly.

They care as much about judgment as software: finding the story rather than following a shot list, restraint in pacing and sound, accurate time estimates, and keeping a project moving when direction is incomplete.
