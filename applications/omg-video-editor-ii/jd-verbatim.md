About the Role
OMG is seeking a highly qualified Video Editor II who can independently carry commercial projects through the complete post-production lifecycle - from ingest and organization through final finishing, revisions, export, and client-ready delivery. This is not an entry-level editing position, and it is not limited to assembling short-form content. The right editor combines confident storytelling and visual judgment with strong technical finishing skills across editing, sound, music, color, motion graphics, and basic visual effects. You should be comfortable making decisions, solving problems, working with incomplete direction when necessary, and consistently delivering polished work that serves each client's brand, audience, objectives, and product.

As a Video Editor II, you will own assigned projects throughout post-production with minimal supervision, identifying the strongest and most efficient way to showcase each project while protecting the client's vision, factual accuracy, brand standards, delivery requirements, and the high level of creative quality expected from OMG. Editor II represents full operational independence: you are expected to understand the intent of a project, anticipate problems, communicate realistic timelines, execute revisions efficiently, and deliver work that is ready for internal or client approval. A genuine interest in the firearms, tactical, sporting, or outdoor space is critical.

This is an in-person position based out of our office in north Phoenix, Arizona.
Please do not apply if you are not able to work in the office five days a week.
Learn more about us at [www.offensivegroup.com](https://www.offensivegroup.com)

Key Responsibilities

* Independently carry assigned projects from media ingest through final client delivery
* Ingest, organize, label, transcode, and manage footage and project assets using OMG file structures, workflows, backup practices, and SOPs
* Review briefs, footage, brand information, product details, and creative direction to determine the strongest editorial approach
* Build rough cuts, refined edits, and final deliverables using purposeful shot selection, visual context, pacing, momentum, energy, music, and sound
* Translate client goals and audience needs into a clear story while maintaining brand voice and product accuracy
* Perform color correction and grading using scopes, color-management principles, and appropriate technical standards
* Execute audio cleanup, sound design, mixing, bus routing, and basic mastering using EQ, compression, limiting, gating, reverb, and related tools
* Create and integrate motion graphics in Adobe After Effects across different visual styles
* Complete basic VFX work and integrate supplied CGI, graphics, or compositing elements when required
* Manage internal and client revisions accurately, efficiently, and without losing alignment with the project's original objective
* Complete a detailed quality-control review before submitting work, including picture, sound, spelling, product information, continuity, specifications, and export settings
* Export, version, upload, organize, and deliver final assets according to platform and client specifications
* Maintain reliable communication, accurate time projections, deadline visibility, and clear project status across concurrent assignments
* Research unfamiliar products, companies, individuals, terminology, and claims to avoid misinformation, misrepresentation, or misinterpretation
* Identify workflow inefficiencies and recurring pain points, then propose practical improvements
* Research and test new editing tools, techniques, effects, and styles that can improve OMG's creative capabilities or production efficiency
* Collaborate closely with Production, Creative, Account Management, and other Post-Production team members
