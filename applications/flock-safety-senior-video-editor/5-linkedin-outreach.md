# LinkedIn Outreach — Senior Video Editor, Flock Safety

Flock is hiring at scale, which means the Film & Editorial team is small, new, and findable. This is their **first full-time dedicated editor** — so whoever is building that team is actively thinking about this hire right now. Outreach lands unusually well in that window.

Search LinkedIn for **"Flock Safety"**, filter by People, then by title. Atlanta and Boston are the hubs.

## Who to target

1. **Creative Director / Head of Film & Editorial** — the posting says you collaborate directly with creative directors. This is the person whose problem you solve.
2. **Video Producer / Senior Producer** — they shoot, you would cut. They know exactly what the edit bay is missing, and they have the hiring manager's ear.
3. **Creative or Technical Recruiter** — fastest answer on the remote question and the eight-year line.

Seven years building narrative out of unscripted interviews and field footage, plus full post: grading in Resolve, audio in Audition, motion in After Effects. What does the current post workflow look like before this hire lands?

**To a recruiter**

> Hi [Name] — I am interested in the Senior Video Editor role in Film & Editorial. Before I go further: is this position open to remote, or is Atlanta/Boston required? I have seven-plus years cutting documentary brand stories and social-first campaigns, with 200+ delivered videos. Happy to send a reel and a couple of finished narrative pieces.

## Notes

- **Send finished pieces, not just the reel.** The posting explicitly warns against "footage assembled cleanly to music." Every message above points at complete narrative work on purpose. This is the whole game for this role.
- **Lead with the veterans film** when talking to anyone on the creative side.
- The producer message is underrated. Producers who are drowning in unedited footage advocate hard for the editor who understands their problem.
- Recruiter first if relocation is the deciding factor for you.
