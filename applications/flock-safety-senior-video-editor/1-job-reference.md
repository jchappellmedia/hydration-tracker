## Plain-English summary

Flock's **first full-time dedicated editor**, and a senior craft voice inside Film & Editorial. You own the cut from rough to final delivery.

The posting describes two modes, and says they matter equally:

1. **Cinematic documentary** — real people, real stakes, emotional restraint, patience to build something that stays with a viewer.
2. **High-octane social-first** — hard openings, kinetic pacing, platform-native typography and motion.

You take raw field footage and customer interviews and build the narrative. You also own the whole post stack: color, sound design, audio leveling, motion graphics integration, and asset organization, plus multiple format cuts for web, social, events, and executive keynotes.

**It is explicitly not a camera role.** Producers and field crews shoot; you stay in post.
