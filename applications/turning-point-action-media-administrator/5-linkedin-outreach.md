# LinkedIn Outreach — Media Administrator, Turning Point Action

Turning Point Action is headquartered in Phoenix, so the media team is local and findable. Search LinkedIn for **"Turning Point Action"**, filter by People, then narrow to the Phoenix area.

## Who to target

1. **Creative Director / Director of Media / Head of Content** — owns this hire and will judge the work itself.
2. **Senior Video Editor / Video Producer** — your future teammate. Editors respond to editors, and they tell the hiring manager who reached out.
3. **Recruiter / Talent Acquisition** — fastest route to the actual listing and the pay band.

> I cannot browse LinkedIn from here. Send me names and titles and I will tailor each message.

## Messages

**To the Creative Director or Head of Media**

> Hi [Name] — I am applying for the Media Administrator role. I cut both long-form and short-form: 200+ videos delivered at Grand Canyon Education, and a social initiative at Charter One that earned over 20x the engagement of our standard content. I have also built an audience of over a million on my own YouTube channel teaching post-production. Portfolio: lightbox-digital.com. Would welcome a conversation.

**To a video editor on the team**

> Hi [Name] — I just applied for the Media Administrator opening and wanted to say hello to someone actually in the chair. Seven years cutting long-form and short-form in Premiere and Resolve, plus motion graphics in After Effects. Portfolio: lightbox-digital.com. What does the day to day look like on your team?

**To a recruiter**

> Hi [Name] — I am interested in the Media Administrator role at your Phoenix office. Seven years of long-form and short-form editing, with social work that earned over 20x the engagement of standard content. Can you share the salary band and who the role reports to? Happy to send my resume either way.

## Notes

- **Lead with the YouTube channel** when talking to anyone on the media side. A million-plus reach on self-produced long-form is unusual and it is the single most relevant thing you have for a role that names YouTube and Rumble.
- Ask the recruiter for the pay band. The title says "Administrator" and the duties say "editor" — that gap is worth resolving before you invest more.
- One proof point per message. The 20x engagement number is the strongest.
- Do not send all three on the same day. Recruiter first, then the media lead.
