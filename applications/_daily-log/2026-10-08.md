## Why this one matters

This is the strongest overall fit of anything in the tracker. He hits **all four** of their
preferred qualifications — construction and real estate marketing, Meta Ads with
lead-generation results, FAA Part 107, and motion graphics / color grading / sound design.
Hitting a preferred list four for four has not happened on any other posting.

The job is really three jobs: make the content, run the paid marketing, and bring in business
face to face. Most applicants will have one. He has all three, with hard numbers on two
(20x engagement, 200% click-through lift, $10k closed and tracked).

The commute is also the best in the tracker — Mesa 85206 to Gilbert 85297, roughly 20 to 25
minutes, inside his stated 30-minute preference.

## The resume build

Reordered to lead with Lightbox rather than Charter One, because the construction and
referral-relationship work is what this employer is actually buying. Header carries the
construction page first, then the general portfolio, then LinkedIn — the posting says
"applications without work samples will not be reviewed."

Page-fitting took several passes. Raising the font size pushed three bullets into second
lines and spilled to two pages, so the fill came from spacing instead: body stayed at
12.7px / 1.53 and the margins opened (`.job` 11→12, `li` 3.9→4.4, `h2` 12/8→13/8, summary
9→10, `.edu-line` 5→6, header 11→12). One page, no dead space at the bottom.
