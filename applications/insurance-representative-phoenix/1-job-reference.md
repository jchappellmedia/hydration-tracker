# Job Description Reference — Insurance Representative (Phoenix, AZ)

## Application Details
- **Company:** Not named in the posting text — job ID 406813, location code "AZ - PHOENIX AZ - 1A", category Sales. *(Tell me the company and I'll update all documents.)*
- **Role:** Insurance Representative
- **Location:** Phoenix, AZ
- **Structure:** Reports to a Managing Representative, Associate Director, or Managing Director. Weekly and annual objectives set with the supervisor.

## Plain-English Summary
This is a life and health insurance sales job. The rep writes new policies, keeps existing policies active (persistency and conservation), and serves policy owners — but the posting is blunt that selling comes first and service exists to create sales opportunities. The rep builds an annual Marketing Plan, meets regularly with a supervisor in Action Planning Conferences, works without close supervision, and is expected to work evenings and weekends. Success is measured in submitted and placed life and health applications, premium growth, PFP sales interviews, Quality Life Check-ups, and persistency/lapse rates.

## Key Duties
- Write new quality life and health business.
- Conserve existing business and serve policy owners.
- Hit weekly/annual objectives: applications, placements, annualized premium increase, PFP interviews, QLCs, persistency, lapse limits.
- Create and execute action plans and an annual Marketing Plan with supervisor approval.
- Work independently, including evenings and weekends.
- Drive net premium growth for the assigned agency.

## Qualifications
- High school diploma required; business-related bachelor's preferred (marketing, business admin, management).
- 1–3 years insurance sales preferred, **not required**.
- **Life and health insurance license required.**
- Valid driver's license and reliable transportation.
- High goals, persistence, urgency, willingness to commit long hours, professional communication.

## Glossary (their jargon)
- **Persistency:** share of policies that stay active (don't lapse). High persistency = quality selling.
- **Conservation:** saving policies that are about to lapse or cancel.
- **PFP:** Personal Financial Planning sales interview.
- **QLC:** Quality Life Check-up — a structured policy review meeting with existing clients.
- **APC:** Action Planning Conference — recurring goal-setting meeting with your supervisor.
