# LinkedIn Outreach Strategy — Insurance Representative (Phoenix)

The posting says reps report to a **Managing Representative, Associate Director, or Managing Director** — those exact titles are who you want to reach. Once you tell me the company name, search LinkedIn for "[Company] Managing Director Phoenix" and similar.

## Who to target
1. **Managing Director / Associate Director of the Phoenix agency** — this person owns rep hiring and lives on recruiting; they respond to direct messages more than most hiring managers.
2. **Managing Representative** — a first-line supervisor who can champion you.
3. **A current Insurance Representative in the Phoenix office** — ask what the first year really looks like (leads, income, support). Their answer also tells you whether to take the job.

## Message templates

**To the Managing Director / Associate Director**
> Subject: Insurance Representative Applicant — Phoenix
>
> Hi [Name], I applied for the Insurance Representative position with your Phoenix agency. I've run my own business for six years — generated my own leads, kept 5.0-star reviews for over a year, and hit my own revenue goals without anyone watching over me. I also hold a Master's in Marketing, so building and working a marketing plan is familiar ground. I'd welcome a conversation.

**To a Managing Representative**
> Subject: Applied — Insurance Representative
>
> Hi [Name], I just applied to join the Phoenix team as an Insurance Representative. My background is six years of relationship-based selling as a business owner, plus a Master's in Marketing. I'm ready to work the plan, including evenings and weekends. Happy to talk anytime.

**To a current rep**
> Subject: Quick question about the rep role
>
> Hi [Name], I'm applying to be an Insurance Representative in the Phoenix office and would value a working rep's honest take. What's the first year actually like — leads, support, income ramp? Fifteen minutes of your time would mean a lot.

## Tone reminders
- Agency leaders in this model recruit constantly — expect fast replies and a quick pitch back at you. Stay calm and ask your own questions.
- Mention the self-generated leads and the Master's — those separate you from the typical applicant pool.
