Role Overview
The Sr. Paid Social Media Campaign Strategist is a high-impact, hands-on, strategic campaign manager who will own, and scale Trajan's paid social strategy across Meta, LinkedIn, and YouTube. The ideal expert in this role is the engine behind our digital lead generation - responsible for growing qualified leads significantly while driving down cost per lead.
Reporting directly to the CMO, this role carries significant autonomy to architect campaigns, optimize spend, and recommend budget reallocation across channels as performance data warrants. You will also contribute to content production - specifically short-form creative for Meta Reels and YouTube - but media buying expertise is the non-negotiable foundation of this role. Content skills are a meaningful plus, not the primary requirement.
This is a role for someone who is 100% proficient in Meta Ads, deeply analytical, and driven by the measurable growth of a brand they believe in. Must also be excellent at reporting results and hungry to do so.
