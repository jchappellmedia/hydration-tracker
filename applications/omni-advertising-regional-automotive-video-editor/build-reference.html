Company Info

Omni Advertising is a full-funnel performance agency that drives sales for automotive dealer associations and Tier III auto dealerships nationwide. The team uses industry experience, digital strategy, and data-driven solutions to deliver measurable results.

What You'll Do

  • Edit high-quality television commercials, digital content, social media, and promotional videos.
  • Collaborate with producers, writers, creative directors, and account teams from concept through final delivery.
  • Bring creative ideas to the table while maintaining strong technical execution.
  • Manage multiple projects and deadlines without sacrificing quality.
  • Ensure all deliverables meet broadcast and digital specifications.

What They're Looking For

  • 5+ years of professional editing in an agency, production company, or post-production environment.
  • Experience editing campaigns for National and/or Regional Automotive clients (required).
  • Expert knowledge of Adobe Premiere Pro, Adobe Creative Suite, After Effects, and motion graphics.
  • Strong sense of pacing, storytelling, music, and visual composition.
  • Ability to balance creative excellence with fast turnaround times.
  • Strong attention to detail and organization.
  • Team player with a positive attitude and strong communication.

Bonus Points

  • Color correction, motion graphics, or sound design.
  • Familiarity with AI-assisted creative workflows.

Benefits

401(k) matching · Health, dental, vision insurance · Health savings account · Life insurance · Paid time off · Parental leave

Company research

Omni Advertising is an automotive ad agency in Boca Raton, Florida. Directory listings say it was founded in 2001 and has about 100-250 employees. It describes itself as a full-funnel performance agency for Honda dealer associations and Tier 3 (individual local) auto dealers nationwide. Its services include media buying, search, social, and video and connected-TV advertising. There aren't many employee reviews: about 25 on Glassdoor and 5 on Indeed. They cover the whole company, not the video team. Most come from the Boca Raton office and run from about 2016 to 2025. Only a few are from creative roles, and none clearly come from remote editors. Watch the name: Omnicom, Omni Digital Group (Iowa), Omni Interactions and other 'Omni' companies show up in searches and are not this employer.

What employees say: pros and cons

Pros

  • Coworkers and team culture get the most praise. Reviewers mention a friendly team, people treating each other with respect, and making friends at work. [1] [2]
  • Reviewers describe flexible, accommodating supervisors, and some mention remote work. Indeed rates work-life balance 4.4 of 5, but that is only 5 reviews. [1] [2]
  • You can learn if you ask. One reviewer says that if you tell them an area you want to learn more about, they make it happen. [1]
  • It is an established agency with a steady niche: founded in 2001, focused on Honda dealer associations and Tier 3 dealers. Its listing says it represents 10 of the top 25 volume Honda dealers. [1] [2]
  • Video is a real service line, not a side job. Video and connected-TV marketing are listed services, and a Honda dealer case study credits their display and video campaigns with a 28% rise in video completion rate. [1]
  • A creative reviewer close to this role (2021 motion graphics artist) called it fun, with stressful moments, but a great place to work. [1] [2]

Cons

  • Pay is the most consistent complaint. Several Glassdoor reviews say pay is below market, compensation and benefits rates about 2.7-2.8 on Glassdoor and 3.0 on Indeed, and a 2019 copywriter said pay did not fit Boca Raton's cost of living. [1] [2]
  • Your experience depends on your manager. Reviews mention micromanagement (2024 project manager), account managers who micromanage, and a lack of structure. [1] [2]
  • The workload can be heavy. Some days need longer hours, one account coordinator said the work is more than one person can handle, and the Boca Raton Glassdoor page rates work/life balance about 2.9. [1] [2]
  • Expect high-pressure agency work. Reviewers say advertising here is high-pressure and takes time from everyone, and the role itself asks for fast turnarounds across many dealer projects. [1]
  • Career growth is the lowest-rated category on Glassdoor (about 3.2), and some reviewers say growth is limited in certain roles. [1] [2]
  • One harsh 2023 review criticizes the owner and department heads, including claims of unpaid bonuses and long hours. It is a single unverified review, but it is worth asking how bonuses work. [1]

Watch for

  • A single 2023 Glassdoor review alleges unpaid bonuses. It is unverified, but if a bonus is offered, get the terms in writing.
  • Very few reviews (about 25 on Glassdoor, 5 on Indeed). Most are 2016-2024 and come from Boca Raton office staff, so they say little about remote editors today.
  • The company is in Boca Raton, Florida (Eastern Time). A remote editor in Mesa would likely work 2-3 hours behind the team, so ask what hours they expect.
  • The posting went up July 13, 2026, about three months ago, and no live copy could be found. It may be filled or closed.
  • Name confusion: Omnicom layoff news, Omni Digital Group (Iowa) video editor jobs and Omni Interactions reviews show up in searches but belong to other companies.

Bottom line

This looks like a mid-sized, established auto-dealer agency where people like their coworkers and the flexibility. The steady complaints are pay below market and a lot depending on which manager you get. The sample is small and old, so don't lean hard on the star ratings. Before accepting, ask who the editor reports to, how many spots a week they expect, normal turnaround times, what hours a remote editor in Arizona should keep, where in the $60k-$100k range they plan to pay, and whether any bonus is in writing.
