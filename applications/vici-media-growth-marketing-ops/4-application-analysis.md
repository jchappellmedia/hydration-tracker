# Application Analysis & Feedback — Growth Marketing & Operations Partner, Vici Media

## Job Match: Partial — with the best single hook of any job in your tracker

## The hook

They wrote: *"You don't just know what AI is; you leverage advanced prompting and AI Workflows daily."* Then they made the application itself a test of it — a two-paragraph response about your favorite AI workflow.

Almost every applicant will answer that with "I use ChatGPT to help write copy." You will answer with **Claude agentic coding that cut production times in half at a real employer**, plus a 30% production reduction across your own company's pipeline. That is a materially better answer than the field will produce, and they built the application to reward exactly that.

The draft is in `6-ai-workflow-response.md`. Submit it as written — it is the strongest asset in this application.

## The hard gate

**"Hands-on proficiency with Sprout Social and Google Analytics is strictly required."**

You have neither documented:
- **Sprout Social** — nowhere in your background. You have run social and driven 20x engagement, but not on their platform.
- **Google Analytics** — your Marketing Analytics specialization and Google certification cover the concepts; hands-on GA reporting is not documented.
- **Salesforce** — also absent, though they soften that one ("full admin background not required").

That is three named platforms, two of them called "strictly required." A screening recruiter may filter on them. Neither appears on your resume, and neither should — you cannot demo a tool you have not used.

**What you can honestly say:** the analytical work these platforms serve is work you already do. You track campaign performance, run Meta ads with pixel-based attribution, and hold a Master's specialization in Marketing Analytics. Sprout Social is a scheduling and listening interface, not a discipline; Google Analytics is a week of self-teaching for someone with your analytics coursework. Say that plainly rather than implying experience.

## Two honest flags

1. **Pay sits at your floor.** $65k–$85k midpoints to exactly $75,000. Your Master's and six years put you at the top of that band, not the middle — negotiate there. The quarterly bonus is real upside but unquantified.
2. **"Right-hand to leadership" roles vary widely.** They say explicitly it is not admin-heavy, which is a good sign. Still worth asking in the interview what percentage of a typical week is marketing versus operations support, and what happened to the last person in the role.

## Close the gap this week
1. **Google Analytics 4 certification** — free, and directly answers a "strictly required" item: https://skillshop.docebosaas.com/pages/59/analytics-academy
2. **Sprout Social** — they offer a free trial and Sprout's own training library. Even a few hours of hands-on gives you an honest answer instead of a blank.
3. **Salesforce Trailhead** — free, and the reports-and-dashboards module is short: https://trailhead.salesforce.com/

Doing GA4 alone converts this from "missing two required platforms" to "missing one," and it is a weekend.

## Bottom line
Apply, and lead with the AI response — that is what gets you read. Go in knowing the platform gate is real and that the pay tops out near your floor. Of everything in your tracker, this is the job where your most distinctive skill is the exact thing they are shopping for.
