# Job Description Reference — Growth Marketing & Operations Partner, Vici Media

## Application Details
- **Company:** Vici Media
- **Location:** Remote (US time zones preferred)
- **Type:** Full-time
- **Pay:** $65,000 – $85,000/year + quarterly performance bonus tied to role and department goals
- **Source:** ZipRecruiter (posting text pasted; the link itself is unreachable from this environment)
- **Application requires:** Resume **plus** a 2-paragraph response describing a favorite AI tool or prompt workflow. See `6-ai-workflow-response.md`.

## Plain-English Summary
A dual role: half growth marketing, half operations right-hand to leadership. On the marketing side you own campaigns end to end, run the corporate social presence through Sprout Social, audit performance in Google Analytics, build Salesforce dashboards for pipeline visibility, and embed AI tools into the workflow. On the operations side you translate leadership's loose ideas into concrete plans, remove bottlenecks, and manage priorities before they escalate.

They are explicit that this is execution-heavy, not admin support, and that they want high autonomy — someone who decodes the vision rather than waits for instructions.

## Key Responsibilities
**Growth marketing**
- Own internal campaigns from concept to execution with brand consistency.
- Scale corporate social presence via **Sprout Social** — scheduling, listening, audience growth, engagement tracking.
- Audit digital performance in **Google Analytics**; build attribution models and optimize ROI.
- Build and manage **Salesforce** dashboards and reports for lead flow, conversions, pipeline visibility.
- Experiment with and integrate **AI tools** for content, reporting, data tracking, and pipeline optimization.

**Strategic operations**
- Turn big-picture strategy and loose concepts into structured project plans.
- Own ad-hoc special projects; find bottlenecks and streamline internal workflows.
- Track deliverables, manage shifting priorities, resolve hurdles before executive intervention.

## Qualifications
- **2–5 years** blended performance digital marketing, growth operations, or strategic support
- **Hands-on Sprout Social and Google Analytics — "strictly required"**
- Functional Salesforce report/dashboard experience (admin/coding not required)
- Practical AI workflow experience: automated reporting, programmatic content generation, prompt engineering
- Strong written/verbal communication; poise with leadership

## What They Offer
Total ownership of domains · performance incentives on department goals · freedom to adopt new marketing tech and AI stacks · direct proximity to leadership and mentorship

## Company
Vici Media — described only as "a dynamic, fast-paced, high-growth collaborative workplace."
