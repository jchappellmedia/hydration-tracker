# LinkedIn Outreach Strategy — Vici Media

The posting says the role works in "direct collaboration and proximity to leadership," which usually means a small team where the founder or a VP reads applications personally. Outreach has real leverage here.

> Verify which Vici Media this is before messaging — the name is used by more than one company. Match the "dynamic, fast-paced, high-growth" description and a remote-friendly US team.

## Who to target
1. **Founder / CEO / President** — this role is explicitly the leadership right-hand, so the hiring decision likely sits with the person you would support.
2. **VP or Director of Marketing** — if one exists, they own the marketing half.
3. **Recruiter or Head of People**.

## Messages

**To the founder or executive this role supports**
> Subject: Growth Marketing & Operations Partner — applied
>
> Hi [Name], I applied for the Growth Marketing & Operations Partner role. The line about leveraging AI workflows daily is what caught me — I use Claude agentic coding as a production system, and it cut our content production times in half at my current employer. Alongside that: a 200% lift in ad click-through rates and six years running my own company, which is where the operations half comes from. I'd welcome a conversation.

**To a marketing lead**
> Subject: Applied — Growth Marketing & Operations Partner
>
> Hi [Name], I submitted my application for the Growth Marketing & Operations role. My background blends campaign execution with AI workflow design — Claude agentic coding that halved production time, a 200% CTR lift, and a Master's in Marketing Analytics. Happy to walk through the actual workflow if useful.

**To a recruiter**
> Subject: Growth Marketing & Operations Partner application
>
> Hi [Name], I applied for the Growth Marketing & Operations Partner position. Six-plus years across campaign execution and growth operations, a Master's in Marketing Analytics, and hands-on AI workflow experience that produced measurable time savings. Available to talk any time this week.

## The move that matters most
Offer to **screen-share the workflow**. For a team that built AI fluency into their application, seeing a real system beats reading about one — and it is the fastest way to make the missing Sprout Social and Google Analytics line items feel small.

## Tone
Their copy is high-energy and buzzword-dense. Do not mirror it. Calm specifics read as more credible next to their own writing, and specifics are what you have.
