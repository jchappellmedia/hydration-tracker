Social Content Creator


Salary Range: $85,000 to $100,000 per year

Onsite Location: Scottsdale, AZ

Workplace Location: Fully Onsite


POSITION SUMMARY

As a Social Media Content Creator you will independently capture, edit, organize, and help produce high-quality long-form and short-form content for our brands, campaigns, events, podcasts, and executive thought leadership.

This is a film-heavy, editing-forward role for a skilled creator who understands professional content capture, visual storytelling, and modern social media. You will film across studio shoots, executive sessions, events, travel, and day-to-day brand opportunities while partnering with Social Media and Production to deliver polished, platform-ready content.

The ideal candidate is confident behind the camera and in the edit. They can operate equipment independently, make strong creative decisions in real time, capture footage with the final story in mind, and turn raw footage into high-performing content that is ready for distribution.

You are proactive, highly organized, visually skilled, and comfortable working in fast-moving production environments. You bring a strong point of view while remaining collaborative, adaptable, and committed to delivering work that meets a high creative standard.

SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE

Founders, executives, and internal teams have a seamless, professional filming experience because shoots are prepared, organized, and executed with care.
Long-form, short-form, podcast, event, and executive content is captured at a consistently high visual standard.
Raw footage is thoughtfully captured with the final edit in mind, giving editors and social teams the angles, coverage, B-roll, and visual variety needed to create strong-performing content.
Assigned edits are delivered as polished, platform-native assets that align with brand standards and content objectives.
Footage is organized, labeled, and delivered in a way that allows editors, vendors, and internal teams to quickly locate and use assets.
The creator proactively identifies opportunities to improve framing, lighting, camera angles, audio, visual details, pacing, storytelling, and production quality.
Same-day event content and social-first assets are captured, edited, and delivered quickly when needed.
The creator brings strong editorial judgment and production insight that strengthens content performance across platforms.

OBJECTIVES

Partner with Social Media and Production teams to execute the creative vision for executive, brand, podcast, event, campaign, and social content.
Independently capture high-quality long-form and short-form video content, including teleprompter-based videos, talking-head content, interviews, podcasts, B-roll, behind-the-scenes footage, vlogs, event coverage, and social-first assets.
Operate cameras, lighting, audio, teleprompters, and related production equipment during studio shoots, on-location shoots, executive sessions, and live events.
Prepare for shoots by reviewing production direction, understanding content goals, following shot lists, and anticipating the coverage needed for final edits.
Proactively identify additional footage, angles, visual moments, and storytelling opportunities that strengthen the final product.
Independently edit polished short-form and select long-form video content, including social clips, trailers, recaps, podcast promotions, executive thought-leadership content, announcements, and timely brand opportunities.
Create engaging, platform-native edits with strong pacing, visual storytelling, music, sound design, captions, basic motion graphics, color correction, and formatting appropriate for each channel.
Collaborate with editors, vendors, and internal teams while owning assigned projects from raw footage through final delivery, including selects, B-roll, revisions, and delivery specifications.
Organize, label, upload, and archive footage in a highly structured manner using approved asset-management systems.
Leverage AI technologies and workflow tools to improve production efficiency, organization, editing, and team collaboration.
Stay current on social media trends, filming techniques, editing styles, camera technology, lighting, and platform-native content practices.
Support content capture during travel and live events as needed.

Advanced experience producing, filming, and editing video content for digital platforms.
Strong understanding of camera operation, framing, composition, visual storytelling, lighting, and audio.
