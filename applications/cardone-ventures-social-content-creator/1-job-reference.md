## Plain-English summary

They want one person who can both shoot and cut. You film studio shoots, executive sessions, podcasts, events, and travel, then edit the footage yourself into finished, platform-ready social content. You are expected to work alone with the gear, make creative calls live on set, and shoot with the final edit already in your head.

The role sits between the Social Media team and the Production team. Some edits you own start to finish; other footage you hand off, so file organization and labeling matter as much as the shooting.

Volume and speed are the point. Same-day event turnarounds come up. They also want someone using AI tools to work faster.
