Company research

Muck Rack makes PR and communications software: a media/journalist database, media monitoring, and coverage reporting. It is self-funded and has been remote-first for over a decade. Its job postings say it has pledged to stay fully distributed. Headcount estimates range from about 290 (TrueUp) to 478 (Revelio Labs, 2025). Sacra estimates ARR at $108M in April 2026, up about 25% year over year; this is a third-party estimate. Muck Rack bought Keyhole, a social listening and analytics tool, in August 2024 and Ruepoint, a media intelligence firm, in January 2025. Gregory Galant is still listed as co-founder and CEO, and no 2026 CEO change turned up. The Social Media Manager role (Built In job 11448070) is fully remote in the US and pays $97K-$120K plus a quarterly bonus. It covers B2B SaaS social strategy, paid social, employee advocacy, social listening, and crisis/reputation work across LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, and X, with up to 10% travel and an annual offsite. An earlier Built In posting for the same title (job 3313829) was for the company's first-ever Social Media Manager and was taken down on Nov 18, 2024, so the current opening may be a backfill. Most employee reviews are company-wide or from sales roles. Only 3 Glassdoor reviews are tagged Marketing, and several of those date from 2020-2022. What employees say: pros and cons

Pros

  • Remote-first for over a decade, with a stated pledge never to force staff back into an office. The company was named to Built In's 2024 Best Places to Work in remote categories. Glassdoor rates work-life balance about 4.3 of 5. [1] [2] [3] [4]
  • Reviewers across Glassdoor praise coworkers as friendly and collaborative. Some call it the best company they have worked for in years. [1] [2] [3]
  • Benefits include unplugged vacation time and a 401(k) with employer contributions. A 'select' health plan covers 100% of the premium for individuals and 85% for dependents. Remote staff get a home office stipend, coworking memberships, and internet and phone reimbursement. [1] [2] [3]
  • US pay is geo-neutral: it is set by job level and function, not location, and reviewed at least once a year. The Social Media Manager role lists $97K-$120K plus a quarterly bonus. [1] [2] [3]
  • The product has a strong reputation, which helps a social role selling to PR and comms buyers. Employees call it one of the few reputable PR platforms, and G2 shows 4.6 from 392 product reviews. The Keyhole acquisition adds social listening tools that match the role's social listening and reputation duties. [1] [2] [3]
  • No known layoffs per TrueUp. Revelio Labs shows 2.3% headcount growth from 2024 to 2025. Muck Rack calls itself self-funded, and Sacra estimates ARR growth of about 25% to $108M in April 2026. [1] [2] [3] [4]
  • The few marketing-tagged Glassdoor reviews are positive (3 reviews, 4.9 average). They cite remote work, benefits, internal transparency, and the chance to move between departments. The sample is small and partly old. [1]

Cons

  • Reviewers report a turnover spike in 2025, including 'many well-respected managers and VPs' across the go-to-market org. Marketing often sits in that org at SaaS companies. [1] [2] [3]
  • Favoritism and politics come up repeatedly: promotions are tied to loyalty to leadership, people are let go for dissenting views, and leadership ignores feedback from high performers. One July 2026 review says this outright. [1] [2] [3] [4]
  • One 2-star sales review says leadership is cutting headcount through performance pressure and quiet exits instead of transparent layoffs. Revelio Labs also shows active job postings down 58% in 2025. This could mean slower hiring, but it is not a confirmed cut. [1] [2]
  • Career growth is the lowest-rated Glassdoor category (about 3.5-3.6). Reviewers describe unclear advancement paths, including a marketing reviewer, and slowed upward mobility for SDRs. [1] [2] [3]
  • Pay and benefits complaints: compensation and benefits rate about 3.7. Raises for internal promotions reportedly lag the external market, one marketing reviewer said pay needed updating, and health insurance is described as 'not particularly amazing.' [1] [2] [3]
  • Workload and availability: a marketing reviewer says Slack can feel 'always on,' and another reviewer says staff can be expected to do the jobs of 2-3 people. Both matter for a solo social role that also covers crisis management. [1] [2]
  • The sales org appears strained. RepVue reports about 25-34% of reps hitting quota against a 44% industry average, and a culture and leadership score of 3.3. The Indeed reviewer claims about 10% of AEs made quota. Pressure on revenue can reach marketing and demand-gen teams. [1] [2] [3]

Watch for

  • Several Glassdoor reviews from 2025-2026 describe go-to-market turnover, favoritism, and poor mid-level leadership. The newest critical review found is from July 2026, so this is a current issue. Ask which org the social role reports into and how long its leader has been there.
  • One review alleges headcount is being cut through performance pressure and quiet exits instead of announced layoffs. TrueUp lists no known layoffs, so ask directly about attrition and any reductions in 2025-2026.
  • Muck Rack posted its first-ever Social Media Manager role in 2024 (taken down Nov 18, 2024) and is hiring for the title again in 2026. Ask whether this is a backfill, what happened to the previous hire, and whether the role is a team of one.
  • The role lists crisis management and reputation monitoring, and a marketing reviewer says Slack feels 'always on.' Ask about after-hours and weekend coverage expectations.
  • Low sales quota attainment (RepVue 25-34% vs 44% industry average) can mean pressure on marketing to produce pipeline. Ask how social success is measured (brand vs pipeline metrics).
  • The marketing-specific evidence is thin: only 3 Glassdoor reviews, several from 2020-2022. Most negative detail comes from sales roles. Ask to speak with a current marketing team member.
  • Indeed's 1.0 rating rests on one review, and the Indeed connector was unavailable, so the Indeed rating could not be verified.

Bottom line

For a remote US social media role, Muck Rack has real strengths: a long record as a remote-first company with a pledge to stay that way, geo-neutral pay of $97K-$120K plus a quarterly bonus, solid benefits, a well-known product, and apparent revenue growth with no reported layoffs. Overall reviews sit around 4.0 on Glassdoor. The main risk is a cluster of 2025-2026 reviews describing go-to-market turnover, favoritism, and weak leadership, though most of those come from sales. Marketing-specific reviews are few and partly old, so they do not settle how the marketing team runs today. Before accepting, Joshua should ask who the role reports to and how long that person has been there, whether this is a backfill of the 2024 first-ever social hire, how big the team is, what after-hours crisis coverage is expected, and how success will be measured.
