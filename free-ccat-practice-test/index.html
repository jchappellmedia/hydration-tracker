Why practice at all?

Because the CCAT isn't really testing what you know — it's testing how fast you handle questions you've never seen. 18 seconds each is brutal the first time and much less brutal the third time. That gap is the whole reason practice works. You're not getting smarter; you're getting familiar, and familiar is faster.

It also matters that you usually only get one shot. Most employers don't let you retake it for the same application, so the practice has to happen before the real thing.

How this test is built

The practice test follows the same blueprint as the real CCAT — about 18 verbal questions, 21 math and logic, and 11 spatial, mixed together and getting harder as you go. The questions are original, written to match the real test's style and difficulty. Nobody can legitimately give you the actual questions, and you should be suspicious of anyone claiming to.
